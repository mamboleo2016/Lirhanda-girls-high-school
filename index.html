About Us

A School Built on Integrity & Ambition

Founded in 1913 as Kenya’s first Friends (Quakers) Primary Boarding School and re-established as a Girls’ Secondary School in 1969, Lirhanda Girls is a 4-stream County School with 413 students and 33 qualified teachers, set on 8.37 hectares in the verdant hills of Shinyalu, Kakamega County.

📚

Academic Excellence

33 qualified teachers (28 TSC, 5 BOM) delivering 16 KCSE subjects and CBC pathways across 15 classrooms, a library, 3 science labs, and a computer lab.

💻

ICT & Technology

A dedicated computer/ICT laboratory offering hands-on training in Computer Studies and CBC Computer Science, preparing girls for a digital economy.

🌱

Character Development

Co-curricular activities, mentorship programmes, and pastoral care cultivate leadership, teamwork, and civic responsibility.

🤝

Supportive Community

A fully boarding school with 8 dormitories (capacity 500), a sickbay, sports grounds for hockey, volleyball, soccer and netball, and 12 staff houses on campus.

Our Story

Brief History

Over a century of education, faith, and service to Kenya.

1913
Founded

Lirhanda School established as the first Friends (Quaker) Primary Boarding School in Kenya, headed by Miss Roxie Reeves.

1962
Transfer to Kaimosi

The school was transferred to Kaimosi to become Kaimosi Girls Boarding School.

1963
Return to Lirhanda

Lirhanda Church elders decided to bring back the school. Miss Peal Nancy Spoon brought the school back from Kaimosi.

1964
Intermediate School

Re-opened as an intermediate school, supported by missionaries Edgar T. Hole (Khavele), Roxies Reeves, Rakama, Arthur B. Chilson (Shikanga) and Parker N. Fred (Vuledi).

1969
Girls’ Secondary School

Under the leadership of Mrs. Robai Andega, Lirhanda Girls’ Secondary School was officially started — the chapter that continues to this day.

📢
Curriculum Transition

Lirhanda Girls currently runs two curricula simultaneously. Form 3 & 4 follow the 8-4-4 KCSE programme — our Form 3 cohort (77 students) will be among the last students nationally to sit KCSE in 2027. Grade 10 students follow the new CBC curriculum with 24 learning pathways. From 2028, the school will run exclusively under CBC (Grade 10–12).

🕊️ Friends (Quakers) Sponsored

Rooted in Quaker Values

Lirhanda Girls High School is proudly sponsored by the Friends Church (Quakers), a community known for its commitment to peace, integrity, equality, and simplicity. These values shape our school culture and pastoral care, supported by our school chaplain and the Christian Union. We nurture the whole person — academically, spiritually, and socially.
